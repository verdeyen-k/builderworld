How to play

  • You start next to your 🏰 centre tower in a world that goes on forever. Walk with WASD / arrow keys, or the joystick on a phone.
  • Tap 🌲 trees, 🪨 rocks, 🔩 metal scraps and 🫐 berry bushes next to you to gather. Hold to keep gathering.
  • Stuff only counts once it reaches the tower. Walk your backpack there, or build harvesters and run conveyor belts into the tower.
  • Tame animals with berries: 🐺 + 5🫐 = 🐕 dog (fights monsters), 🐀 + 2🫐 = 🐁 tamed rat (every rat makes harvesters 5% faster), 🐔 + 7🫐 = 🐓 chicken (every chicken makes dogs 5% stronger), 🦉 + 9🫐 = owl (follows you and heals you and your dogs nearby).
  • At night monsters come for the tower. Build 🧱 walls and turrets before dark!
